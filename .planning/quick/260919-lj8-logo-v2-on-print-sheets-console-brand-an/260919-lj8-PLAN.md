Founder request 19 Sep 2026, following quick-260919-kel.

## Diagnosis: why the favicon still looks old
Live head tags are correct (two media-scoped SVGs, .ico fallback, apple-touch). Two real gaps: (1) `/favicon.ico` and `/apple-touch-icon.png` at the ROOT return 404 on both hosts — crawlers (Google's favicon in search results), link unfurlers and some browsers request those paths directly and ignore <link>; this has been an open carry-forward since Phase 9. (2) `/brand/favicon.ico` kept its URL across the rebrand, and browsers cache favicons far longer than pages — a returning visitor keeps v1 until the URL changes.
