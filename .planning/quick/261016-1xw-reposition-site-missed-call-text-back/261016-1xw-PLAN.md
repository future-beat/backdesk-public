# Quick task: reposition the site around missed-call text-back

Founder decision, 16 Oct 2026: the headline is that Backdesk texts back every missed call in seconds and chases it until it is booked; quote recovery is the second line; the free Quote Leakage Audit stays the door (CTA unchanged). v1.2 shipped the text-back product (Phases 11 to 15); the public site still sells only the quote cadence. This task moves the words. Brand, prices and tier names, the Spam Act and privacy sentences, the 3× guarantee (now explicitly about recovered quote value) and the one AI disclosure phrase all stay.

## Decisions the executor pastes rather than makes

**The h1 is `Every missed call texted back in seconds.`** It is the first clause of the founder's approved headline verbatim, so it introduces no new claim; it says the mechanism (text back) and the speed, which is the differentiator; it is 41 characters, the same length as the line it replaces, so the hero at 390px and the OG image's 900px two-line box need no layout work; and its "Every ..." shape makes the lede's quote line ("Every quote you send is chased ... until it's won or dead.") read as the matched pair, which is the founder's long form split across h1 and lede. "Miss the call. Keep the job." was considered and rejected: the imperative reads as advice to miss calls, and "keep the job" promises an outcome a text-back cannot guarantee, which the guarantee section deliberately does not promise either.

**"Reported in dollars" merges into step 4, not the guarantee.** The steps are the sequence a client lives through and the monthly page is the last thing in that sequence; the guarantee section already names the report's two figures (gross and conservative) and is about the conditional, not the artefact. Step 4's title becomes "Every open quote chased, reported in dollars" so the scannable phrase survives, and its body ends with the report sentence, now including the missed-call line the Phase 15 report carries.

**The fit paragraph drops the "fewer than about 8 quotes a month" exclusion** (planner's discretion, stated here): a trade that misses calls now has a reason to buy regardless of quote volume, so the quote-count floor moves onto the quote-side list ("sending 10 or more quotes a month") instead of being a reason to turn someone away.

**Found during planning: the OG PNG bakes the tagline in.** `scripts/brand/og-image.html:124` paints the old line and `public/brand/og-image.png` is the committed render, while `layout.tsx:61` derives the og:image alt from `TAGLINE`. Moving the constant without re-rendering would ship a preview whose alt text and picture disagree, so Task 2 re-renders (Chrome is present at the script's default path) and pins the HTML to the constant.

**`docs/external-registrations.md` has no product sentence to edit.** It is a lead-time and deploy-evidence log; the fitting update is one dated entry in its own convention (what changed on the public site, the deployed SHA and deployment id, the probes after deploy), written in Task 3 when those facts exist.

Not touched, on purpose: `src/app/(marketing)/page.tsx` (section order, six h2, two buttons; the tests pin all three), `src/app/(marketing)/layout.tsx` (the alt follows TAGLINE), `src/app/(marketing)/structured-data.ts` (carries no description), `GUIDE_COPY` and `tests/guide-page.test.ts`, `PRIVACY_COPY`, and `docs/vercel-domains-and-protection.md:475` (a dated log of what the title was then).
