<objective>
Open the last door, last. Every behaviour a text-back depends on is now deployed and inert: the gate, the sweep, the stale window, the open-quote branch, the alarms, the templates and never-text surfaces, the privacy section. This plan widens `MESSAGING_CHANNELS` to include `text_back` — a compile-driven change that forces every gate list, refusal sentence and control to exist — and gives the operator the per-client arm and disarm controls, the seventeen-row checklist with its Text-back live line, and the `text_back_proof` arm.

Purpose: TXT-05 and D-09. Until this plan lands, no code path can enable a text-back row; after it, exactly one path can, and it is gated, audited, per-client and guarded by the privacy predicate.

Flagged assumptions (planner decisions on RESEARCH open questions 10 and 11; the founder may overrule either at UAT): (1) the `text_back` gate reuses `BOTH_CHANNELS` WHOLE — D-09 names "onboarding complete + sm8Connected", and `BOTH_CHANNELS` additionally carries `contactHours` (the after-hours variant is chosen from the window) and `escalationPhone` (D-16 escalates open-quote calls to it), so a client with no contact hours or no escalation phone cannot be armed for text-back; (2) IN-02 (the reconcile's 500-call truncation signal) is left as it is — no plan in this phase edits `src/core/telephony/reconcile.ts`, and CONTEXT binds IN-02 only "if touched".

Output: the widened vocabulary, `armChannel` / `disarmChannel` support for the identity-less per-tenant channel, `ARMING_GATES.text_back`, four checklist keys with the strict `divertVerified`, the console controls and proof slot, and the suites that pin all of it. Ends with the push that makes text-back armable in production.

- Core and repository: `text_back` as a third arming channel, per-client only
