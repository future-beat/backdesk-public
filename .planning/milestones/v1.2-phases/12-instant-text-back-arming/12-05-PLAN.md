<objective>
Replace the tracer's fail-safe arm for open-quote callers (a recorded `quote_open` skip) with the founder's rule of 27 Sep 2026: the call attaches to the quote cadence, the cadence pauses, the caller gets the attested "about your quote" wording once, and the tradie is told on their own phone. Then make a reply to any text-back safe end to end on a lead cadence, so a hot reply reaches the tradie now rather than in Phase 13.

Purpose: TXT-04, with D-16 (who is escalated), D-17 (touch 0, no attribution, the existing reply pause) and D-18 (hot replies on a lead escalate now; nothing may throw on a cadence with no quote).

Flagged assumption (TXT-04 — the edge probe could not classify this requirement, so these are stated rather than derived, and are reported to the founder): (1) the match is on the quote cadence's SNAPSHOT mobile only, not on every contact row of the job; (2) a repeat call from the same open-quote caller gets no second text but is still escalated, subject to the cooldown — unless the 24-hour rule skipped it first, in which case nothing is escalated; (3) the escalation follows the existing path's exemption from contact hours, so an after-hours call from a quote customer reaches the tradie's phone at that hour (a per-client after-hours switch is Phase 13, LEAD-03); (4) the about-quote text counts in the live "sent" metrics and "quotes chased", and never in attribution.

This plan opens with the wave-4 gate (push `main`, CI green on the wave-4 DB-backed suites) and closes with its own push.

Output: the D-08 arm of `sendTextBack`, `src/core/escalation/quote-call.ts`, the touch-0 attribution exclusion, lead-cadence null-safety through inbound, classification, escalation and the console, two DB-backed suites.
