<objective>
Make the synchronous text-back a guarantee and make its failures loud. Three passes join the existing per-minute tick: a sweep that sends any text-back the callback did not, inside a 45-second-to-10-minute window with older calls marked stale; an alert pass that emails the operator about failed or stuck text-backs within the tick; and a balance probe that alerts below A$20 and auto-disarms every armed client below A$5 with an audited system actor.

Purpose: Twilio does not retry a timed-out status callback, so the sweep is the only guarantee (D-01); and the shared ClickSend balance has already hit zero once, so a failed text must never be silent (TXT-06).

Scope of the guarantee, stated so nobody reads ROADMAP criterion 1 ("when the synchronous send fails the per-minute tick sends it on its next pass") as broader than it is: the sweep sends ONLY text-backs that were never claimed — the callback never arrived, or the work before the claim failed or timed out. Once a ledger claim exists, nothing is ever re-sent: a send the provider refused (`failed`) and a send whose outcome is unknown (still `claimed`) are ALERTED to the operator within the tick and never retried, because the caller may already have been texted (D-04, D-10). "The tick sends it" therefore means "the tick sends what was never attempted", and "never silent" covers the rest.

Output: `src/core/leads/{sweep,balance}.ts`, `src/core/alerts/text-back-alerts.ts`, `getAccountBalance`, two new tick seams with twelve enumerated counts, one pure suite and one DB-backed suite.

## Artifacts this phase produces (this plan's share)

- `getAccountBalance` in the ClickSend client; counts-only operator emails
</objective>
