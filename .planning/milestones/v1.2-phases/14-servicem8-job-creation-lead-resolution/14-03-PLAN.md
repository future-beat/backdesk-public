<objective>
Close the lead the tradie booked himself, and keep lead-born jobs out of the dollars. On every sync cycle, after the cycle's own transaction commits, a post-commit pass matches each open, untouched lead against the jobs ServiceM8 shows for that client in the fourteen days after the call — by the caller's mobile on the mirrored job contact first, by the company's contacts only for the handful of jobs that have none — and converts the matches `booked_by_tradie` through the existing close path. And a `won` on any job a lead is linked to is `unqualified` before the attribution decision runs, so the operator's tap or the tradie's own booking is counted in leads-to-jobs and never billed.

Purpose: LEAD-04's second half and D-06's "counted, never attributed" are the two rules that make the leads-to-jobs number honest without operator work. Both ride existing machinery — the sync run and the attribution consumer — and both must leave that machinery's suites byte-identical.

Output: `job-match.ts` (pure), `booked-by-tradie.ts`, the one-line hook in `run-sync.ts`, `lead-born.ts` (pure) and the early return in `consume-wins.ts`; two pure suites and one structural pin green locally; `tests/lead-booked-by-tradie.test.ts` written for CI at plan 14-05's gate.

Rules carried: as plan 14-01's objective states. Research's resolved defaults adopted: `SyncCycleResult` is unchanged (OQ3); ServiceM8's job `date` is treated as the account's local calendar date (A1 — an hour's skew matters only at midnight and errs toward one more counted, never attributed, resolution); the test tenant's sync is LIVE (the spike found `paused_at: null`) — the pass runs there on every tick and must spend nothing when a tenant has no open lead.
