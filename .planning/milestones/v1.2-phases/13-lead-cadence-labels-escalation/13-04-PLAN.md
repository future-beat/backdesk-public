<objective>
Give lead replies their own vocabulary and their own hot rule without touching the quote path: STOP first, then a deterministic keyword rule at the door that marks a lead reply hot before any model, then classification into `LEAD_LABELS` plus urgency by a structured output selected by the row's `lead_id`, then a separate tick pass that applies the closing labels to the lead. The D-10 tie-break lands at the same door.

Purpose: LEAD-02 and the keyword half of LEAD-03. The vocabulary must be byte-identical in three places or the CHECK refuses what the model returns; the hot decision for an emergency must not wait on a model; and closing a lead on a label is a state transition the classifier is forbidden to make, so it is its own pass.

Output: `src/core/leads/{urgency-keywords,label-effects}.ts`, the lead arm of the classifier and its schema, the door's keyword mark and tie-break, the `leadsClosedByLabel` tick count, five pure suites and the extended `tests/lead-reply.test.ts` (CI-only).

Rules carried: executor model opus; pure suites named per task run locally; `tests/lead-reply.test.ts` is DB-backed and runs in CI only; never `test:remote`; no env file named; `LEAD_LABELS` byte-identical in three places with a test; the quote vocabulary `INBOUND_LABELS` and every quote classification case untouched; the "must not move" pins proven with `git diff --quiet` against the plan base — the newest commit that touched `13-03-SUMMARY.md`. Research Open Question 4 is adopted as written: the keyword rule has no negation guard, "not urgent" reads hot, and the test pins that with a comment naming the cost (one extra SMS to the tradie, then the model's `flexible` urgency on the row).
