**Goal**: The service presents as Backdesk on every surface a prospect or client sees, on its own domains, with the commercial assumptions the audit and the invoice depend on confirmed and recorded

  1. A prospect opening a Quote Leakage Audit, a client reading a monthly recovered-revenue report, and any recipient of a cadence email or operator alert sees the service named as Backdesk, and the console title and login page read Backdesk — no rendered "Quote Recovery" service-name string remains (the repo/package name may)

### Phase 9: Landing Page & Audit Intake

**Goal**: A prospect can find Backdesk, understand the offer, and request a free Quote Leakage Audit from their phone without an account — and that request reaches the operator's console and phone within a minute
**Depends on**: Phase 8 (Backdesk brand tokens; Vercel domain setup and the deployment-protection exclusions that keep public pages reachable)
**Requirements**: LAND-01, LAND-02, LAND-03, LAND-04, LAND-05, LAND-06
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A prospect on a phone at https://backdesk.com.au (www redirecting) reads the positioning statement, how it works, the Quote Chaser / Pipeline Manager tiers and pricing, the 90-day guarantee, and the AU data-residency and compliance notes
  2. The prospect submits an audit request — business name, contact name, phone, email, trade, ServiceM8 yes/no, approximate quotes per month, optional CSV export — without logging in and sees a confirmation; a honeypot hit or an over-limit IP is refused without a stored request
  3. Within one minute of a submission the operator receives an email and an SMS, and the request is stored tenant-independently (service-role write, RLS-safe) and visible in the console
  4. The operator opens the request in the console and starts a Quote Leakage Audit pre-filled from it in the existing audits/new flow — CSV path when a file was uploaded, API-key path otherwise
  5. The site carries a privacy notice page and contact details (ABN, email, Perth address or PO box) that the form links to before submission, and the landing page scores ≥ 90 Lighthouse performance and accessibility on mobile with correct title, description, Open Graph image and favicon

### Phase 10: First-Client Go-Live

**Goal**: The operator takes a signed founding client from agreement to first cadence send entirely from the console and a runbook, and both live proofs deferred from v1.0 are closed on production
**Depends on**: Phase 8 (verified sending domain for the email proof and for the checklist's "sending domain verified" item; Backdesk sender identification in the proven messages). Does not depend on Phase 9.
**Requirements**: ARM-01, ARM-02, ARM-03, ARM-04, ARM-05, DOM-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. The per-client console checklist shows the full go-live state in one place — templates attested, SM8 automation-off attested, escalation phone set, contact hours set, sending domain verified, escalation handset proof recorded, SMS arming state, email arming state
  2. The operator arms and disarms SMS and email for a client from the console (writing `messaging_config`) with no manual SQL; arming is refused while any checklist item is incomplete, and every arm/disarm is audited with operator, timestamp and reason
  3. On production, a hot-lead reply produces an escalation SMS on the tradie's handset within the tick interval, and the CON-03 proof is recorded
  4. On production, a cadence email leaves the tenant's verified quotes.backdesk.com.au identity through the real sender path, Resend confirms delivery, the received message carries the Backdesk sender identification and the RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, and a live one-click unsubscribe suppresses both channels — the MSG-02 evidence is recorded in the phase verification (reworded 2026-09-11: no inbound-email path exists by design; reply-to is the tradie)
  5. `docs/servicem8-api-notes.md` records the re-measured `sms.json` / `email.json` row shape (D-15 detection updated if it changed), and a `docs/` go-live runbook walks the operator from signed founding agreement to first cadence send, referencing the checklist and both proofs
