# Roadmap: Quote Recovery MVP

## Overview

Seven phases take the project from an empty repo to a running managed service: a tenant-safe foundation (with external lead-time clocks started day 1), a ServiceM8 sync layer that doubles as the Quote Leakage Audit sales instrument, the cadence state machine that IS the product, a compliance-gated messaging gateway, Claude drafting and reply classification, the mobile review console with hot-lead escalation, and finally attribution and reporting that staples proof to the invoice. Phases 1–6 form the 2-week walking skeleton (SMS-only, one tenant, full review, polling-only): quote synced → cadence created → Claude-drafted touch → mobile approval → SMS inside contact hours → reply pauses cadence and escalates to the tradie. Ops hardening and graduated autonomy (research phase P8) are deferred to v2 — sampled review needs weeks of edit-distance data that only exists after launch.

### Phase 2: ServiceM8 Sync & Quote Leakage Audit

**Goal**: Every connected client's open quotes mirror into the system reliably with won/lost detection, and the operator can produce the Quote Leakage Audit from any prospect's data
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: ONB-02, SYNC-01, SYNC-02, SYNC-03, SYNC-04, AUD-01, AUD-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

### Phase 3: Cadence Engine

**Goal**: Every synced open quote gets a deterministic, contact-hours-aware follow-up schedule that structurally cannot send late, twice, or stale
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: CAD-01, CAD-02, CAD-03, CAD-04, CAD-05
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Every synced open quote gets a cadence of up to 5 touches over ~3 weeks with materialized, timezone- and contact-hours-aware send times from the client's template timing
  2. Customer reply, quote won/lost, opt-out, and operator kill switch (per-contact and per-client) each stop or pause the cadence deterministically — proven by exhaustive state-machine tests
  3. A quote can never receive the same touch twice — the send ledger's unique constraint holds under a concurrent-tick test
  4. Dispatch revalidates quote status, suppression list, and send window just-in-time — an enqueue → opt-out → dispatch-refuses test passes
  5. Overdue touches re-window to the next allowed slot instead of sending outside contact hours

### Phase 4: Messaging Gateway

**Goal**: Real SMS and email flow through a single compliance-gated send path, and every inbound message lands in the right conversation
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 3
**Requirements**: MSG-01, MSG-02, MSG-03, MSG-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. SMS sends from a dedicated AU virtual number with delivery receipts recorded per message, and two-way replies arrive back in the system
  2. Email sends from a verified client domain (or service-subdomain fallback) with SPF/DKIM, Spam Act-compliant sender identification, and a functional unsubscribe on every message
  3. STOP/unsubscribe is processed by deterministic regex before any LLM, suppresses the contact across both channels, and takes effect immediately at the send gate
  4. Every outbound message identifies the client business, and every inbound message threads to the correct tenant, contact, and quote — ambiguous routing goes to human triage, never a guess

### Phase 5: AI Drafting & Classification

**Goal**: Every touch is Claude-drafted in the client's approved voice with facts injected from data, and inbound replies classify safely without being able to steer the system
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 2, Phase 3
**Requirements**: AI-01, AI-02, AI-03, AI-04, ONB-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Operator can capture a client voice profile and record client approval of per-touch templates — no cadence can activate before templates are approved
  2. Drafts are generated from templates + merge fields, and a post-generation validator proves every price and date in a draft exists verbatim in source data (failures force mandatory review)
  3. Inbound replies classify into the closed enum (interested / question / objection / not-interested / opt-out / other) via structured output, with low-confidence results routed to the operator
  4. Inbound text cannot alter system behaviour — classification is schema-only with no tools, and its transitions are least-privilege (can pause a cadence, can never opt a contact in)
  5. Every draft records model, template/prompt version, and human edit distance from the very first draft

- **Deferred from research plan:** Research phase P8 (Ops Hardening & Graduated Autonomy — sampled review, webhook subscription management, nightly invariant sweeps, failure drills) is not a v1 phase. Sampled review is explicitly V2-01 (needs 30 days of edit-rate data); webhooks-as-hints architecture (SYNC-03) is built in Phase 2 with subscriptions activated whenever ServiceM8 partner approval lands.
- **Research flags for planning:** Phase 2 (ServiceM8 field-level API, `$filter` syntax, webhook challenge flow, partner-approval mechanics) and Phase 4 (ClickSend inbound-webhook shape, DLR payloads, number lead time, idempotency support; Resend inbound routing) were flagged LOW confidence — use `/gsd-plan-phase --research-phase` for both. - **Evidence-before-reporting invariant:** the send ledger and `cadence_events` audit trail (Phase 3) and delivery/reply records (Phase 4) are the raw material for Phase 7 attribution — they cannot be reconstructed retroactively, so their design is load-bearing in the earlier phases.
