### Phase 11: Telephony Capture

**Goal**: Every call a client diverts to their own Backdesk-controlled Australian number is answered with a short unrecorded greeting and lands as a logged missed call the rest of the milestone can act on — proven on real Telstra, Optus and Vodafone diverts before anything is texted
**Depends on**: Phase 10 (v1.1 shipped — the arming gate, go-live checklist, proof recording and ops probes that Phases 12 and 15 extend); first phase of v1.2
**Requirements**: CAP-01, CAP-02, CAP-03, CAP-04, CAP-05
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. The operator provisions one Australian local (08) Twilio number for a client from the console; it is recorded against that tenant only, is shown on the client's console record, and cannot be shared with another client or released while the client is active (CAP-01)
  2. A caller diverted to that number hears a static greeting of at most 12 seconds — the tradie's business name, that a text is on its way, that the call is not recorded, and that private-number callers should text instead — and is then hung up; no `<Record>`, `<Dial>`, `<Gather>`, transcription or voicemail exists anywhere in the flow, and a test fails if one is introduced (CAP-02)
  3. Every diverted call — including withheld, landline and international callers — becomes exactly one `missed_calls` row (tenant from the called number, caller CLI or a withheld marker, Perth-time start, after-hours flag, provider call id), and a deliberately dropped status callback is recovered by the provider call-log reconcile with no duplicate row (CAP-03)
  4. **Live divert spike (OVERRIDDEN 5 Oct 2026 — skipped by the founder; proof relocated to each client's diverted verification call, see 11-08-SUMMARY.md):** on real no-answer, busy and unreachable diverts from Telstra, Optus and Vodafone handsets the original caller's CLI survives into the stored row (with `ForwardedFrom` fill-rate per carrier recorded as evidence only, never routed on); a `#31#` withheld call is logged with the withheld marker and no number, so it can never be texted (CAP-03)
  5. Both provider webhooks verify the Twilio signature over the canonical public URL plus a per-route token before any write, answer the provider with a uniform greeting/200 on any failure (never 5xx), and log a fixed-string failure the ops probe surfaces; per client, the console shows that a verification call was observed and recorded and the days since the last diverted call (CAP-04, CAP-05)

### Phase 12: Instant Text-back & Arming

**Goal**: Within seconds of a missed call the right caller — and only the right caller — receives one pre-approved text from the client's identity through the existing SMS number and send ledger, and a client can be armed for it only through the go-live gate with a written runbook and a balance alarm behind it
**Depends on**: Phase 11 (missed-call rows, verification call, divert-number record; live divert spike recorded)
**Requirements**: TXT-01, TXT-02, TXT-03, TXT-04, TXT-05, TXT-06, COMP-03, COMP-01 (callers section only, D-11)
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Within seconds of a diverted call ending, the caller receives one SMS from the client's identity via the existing shared number, built from the client's attested static template (business-hours or after-hours variant), at most 2 GSM segments, carrying the Spam Act identity line, STOP and the plain-text privacy pointer, with no LLM in the path; the send sits in `send_ledger` as touch 1 of a `kind='lead'` cadence so delivery receipts, reconcile, STOP suppression and metrics apply exactly as to a cadence touch, and when the synchronous send fails the per-minute tick sends it on its next pass (TXT-01, TXT-02)
  2. Before any send, a deterministic gate skips suppressed numbers, withheld/anonymous callers, non-Australian-mobile numbers, the client's own numbers and the per-client never-text list, recording the skip reason on the missed call; a caller is texted at most once per client per 24 hours and a per-client daily cap (default 30) stops a call flood (TXT-03)
  3. A caller who already has an open quote cadence at the same client gets no new lead: the missed call attaches to that cadence, the cadence pauses, the caller receives the client's "about your quote" template and the tradie's escalation phone is alerted (D-16) — founder decision 27 Sep 2026 (TXT-04)
  4. The operator arms text-back per client only through the existing arming gate, which additionally requires the recorded verification call, an attested text-back template set and the client's own numbers on the never-text list; the go-live checklist shows the item, disarming stops text-backs immediately, every arm/disarm is audited; a ClickSend balance below the alert floor alerts the operator and below the disarm floor auto-disarms text-back, and any failed text-back is surfaced to the operator within the tick (TXT-05, TXT-06)
  5. A per-client onboarding runbook in `docs/` covers the carrier divert codes (61, 62, 67; iPhone settings caveat), the verification call, the carrier-charge warning, a holiday pause state and offboarding (remove the divert before the number is retired) (COMP-03)

### Phase 13: Lead Cadence, Labels & Escalation

**Goal**: A texted caller becomes a lead that is chased briefly and politely — two human-approved follow-ups inside contact hours, then closed for good — with replies classified in a lead vocabulary and the hot ones on the tradie's phone
**Depends on**: Phase 12 (lead row and touch 1 in the ledger; arming)
**Requirements**: LEAD-01, LEAD-02, LEAD-03, LEAD-05
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Each texted caller is a lead with its own `kind='lead'` cadence: the instant text is touch 1 (exempt from contact hours); touches 2 and 3 are AI-drafted from approved lead templates, appear in the existing approval queue for human approval, and are sent inside the client's contact hours over about 48 hours; after touch 3 the lead closes for good and that number is never texted again for that lead — founder decision 27 Sep 2026: one instant text plus two follow-ups, then closed (LEAD-01)
  2. A reply to a lead is classified into the separate lead vocabulary (new_enquiry, callback_request, question, existing_job, complaint, not_a_lead, already_sorted, spam, opt_out, other) with an urgency level and confidence, threads to the lead rather than to a quote cadence, and STOP is handled deterministically before any model exactly as today (LEAD-02)
  3. A hot lead reaches the tradie's phone through the existing escalation path: any emergency-urgency reply, a high-confidence new_enquiry / callback_request / existing_job / complaint, a deterministic `URGENT` keyword reply that bypasses the model, and a second missed call from the same caller within 30 minutes; after-hours escalation is a per-client switch, and the tradie is never texted for every miss — founder decision: `URGENT` keyword in scope (LEAD-03)
  4. Cadence policy (minimum gap, staleness cap, expiry) is chosen per cadence kind — the lead cadence's short timings are not skipped by the quote cadence's 48-hour rules and the quote cadence's behaviour is unchanged — pinned by a test on both kinds (LEAD-05)

### Phase 14: ServiceM8 Job Creation & Lead Resolution

**Goal**: A lead with confirmed intent becomes a ServiceM8 quote job on one operator tap, exactly once, without the sync opening a second cadence on the same customer — and leads the tradie handled himself close honestly without operator work
**Depends on**: Phase 13 (classified replies mark the lead's intent; lead thread). May overlap with Phase 13 once the `leads` table has landed in Phase 12, since the tap and the auto-resolution ride the ServiceM8 client and sync, not the cadence engine
**Requirements**: SM8-01, SM8-02, SM8-03, LEAD-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. From a lead with confirmed intent the operator creates a ServiceM8 job with one tap: status Quote, the caller's own words as the description, the company matched by normalised mobile or created, and a job contact; the console shows the created job only after the new POST path has read the record back from ServiceM8 (SM8-01)
  2. The lead-born job is already in the quotes mirror as `needs_review` with source `lead` when the sync next sees it, so no five-touch quote cadence opens on a customer the lead cadence just chased (SM8-02)
  3. A second tap cannot create a second job for the same lead; a failed creation leaves the lead in a state the operator can retry from, with the ServiceM8 error kind recorded (SM8-03)
  4. A `DONE` reply (or the chosen keyword) from the tradie's own number closes the lead, and a lead whose caller mobile appears on a ServiceM8 job created within 14 days is auto-resolved as "booked by the tradie", so leads-to-jobs is honest without operator work (LEAD-04)
