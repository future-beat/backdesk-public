# Requirements: Quote Recovery MVP — v1.1 GTM Launch (Backdesk)

**Defined:** 2026-09-06
**Core Value:** Every open quote gets followed up on time, with a human-approved message, and every recovered job is attributed and reported in dollars — the invoice must arrive stapled to proof it paid for itself.

**Milestone goal:** Make the delivered loop sellable under the Backdesk brand — a public front door that books Quote Leakage Audits, brand and domains wired end to end, and everything the first paying client needs to go live. Marketing execution (outreach, collateral, partners, case studies) is run in a separate Cowork thread and is not a repo requirement.

## v1.1 Requirements

### Brand

- [x] **BRAND-01**: Every prospect- and client-facing surface (Quote Leakage Audit page, monthly recovered-revenue report, email templates and footers, console title/login page, operator alert emails) identifies the service as **Backdesk**; no "Quote Recovery" service-name string remains in rendered output (the internal repo/package name may stay)
- [x] **BRAND-02**: The three flagged assumptions — `AUDIT_RECOVERY_RATE` (0.15), default baseline win rate (0.70) with `BASELINE_MIN_QUOTES` (10), and setup fee excluded from the recovery multiple — are confirmed by the operator, the confirmed values live in one documented config location, and the decision is recorded in PROJECT.md before any real audit or invoice is produced
- [x] **BRAND-03**: Spam Act sender identification in every outbound email and SMS names the tradie's business as the sender and Backdesk only as the service ("sent by Backdesk on behalf of …"), with the existing functional opt-out unchanged

### Domains & Email

- [x] **DOM-01**: The operator console is served at `https://app.backdesk.com.au` (Vercel custom domain, `syd1` function region unchanged) and the default `*.vercel.app` production URL redirects to it
- [x] **DOM-02**: Vercel Deployment Protection is re-enabled for preview deployments without breaking the pg_cron tick endpoints or any public page (protection bypass/exclusions documented in `docs/`)
- [x] **DOM-03**: `quotes.backdesk.com.au` is verified at Resend (SPF, DKIM, DMARC published; verification status visible in the console email settings page) and is the sending domain for cadence email and operator alerts
- [ ] **DOM-04**: The deferred MSG-02 live email proof is executed end to end on the verified domain in production — a cadence email sent from the tenant's verified `quotes.backdesk.com.au` identity through the real sender path, delivery confirmed at Resend (`last_event=delivered`), Backdesk sender identification and the RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe present in the received message, and a live one-click unsubscribe POST suppressing both channels — with the evidence recorded in the phase verification. (Reworded 2026-09-11: inbound email replies are not threaded to quotes by design — reply-to is the tradie's own address (Phase 4 D-01); inbound email is a v1.2 candidate.)

### Landing Page & Audit Intake

- [x] **LAND-01**: A public landing page at `https://backdesk.com.au` (www redirecting) presents the Backdesk offer — positioning statement, how it works, Quote Chaser / Pipeline Manager tiers and pricing, the 90-day guarantee, AU data residency and compliance notes — and is fully usable on a phone
- [x] **LAND-02**: A prospect can request a free Quote Leakage Audit without logging in, via a form capturing business name, contact name, phone, email, trade, ServiceM8 use (yes/no), approximate quotes per month, and an optional CSV export upload
- [x] **LAND-03**: A submitted request is stored as an audit request (tenant-independent, service-role write, RLS-safe) and the operator is notified by email and SMS within one minute; bot and abuse submissions are limited by a honeypot field and per-IP rate limiting
- [x] **LAND-04**: The operator can open an audit request from the console and start a Quote Leakage Audit pre-filled from it in the existing audits/new flow (CSV path when a file was uploaded, API-key path otherwise)
- [x] **LAND-05**: The public site carries a privacy notice page and contact details (ABN, email, Perth address or PO box) consistent with Privacy Act APP 1/5 expectations and the Spam Act, and the audit form links to it before submission
- [x] **LAND-06**: The landing page passes a basic performance and accessibility check (Lighthouse ≥ 90 performance and accessibility on mobile) and carries correct metadata (title, description, Open Graph image, favicon)

### First-Client Go-Live

- [x] **ARM-01**: The per-client console checklist shows the full go-live state in one place: templates attested, SM8 automation-off attested, escalation phone set, contact hours set, sending domain verified, escalation handset proof recorded, SMS arming state, email arming state
- [x] **ARM-02**: The operator can arm and disarm SMS and email sending for a client from the console (writing `messaging_config`) instead of a manual SQL UPDATE; arming is blocked until every checklist item is complete, and every arm/disarm action is audited with operator, timestamp and reason
- [ ] **ARM-03**: The deferred CON-03 live escalation handset proof is executed on production (hot-lead reply → escalation SMS on the tradie's phone within the tick interval) and recorded
- [x] **ARM-04**: The ServiceM8 `sms.json` / `email.json` row shape is re-measured against a live account and the D-15 SM8-automation detection updated if the shape changed; the measurement is recorded in `docs/servicem8-api-notes.md`
- [x] **ARM-05**: A written first-client go-live runbook in `docs/` walks the operator from signed founding agreement to first cadence send, referencing the console checklist and both live proofs

## Out of Scope

| Trade mark filing | Deferred until incorporation (decision 6 Sep 2026) |
| Self-serve signup, client logins, payments/billing automation | Founder-led sales, manual direct debit for the founding cohort |
| Voice follow-up, lead generation | GTM decisions carried from v1.0 |
| Whole-back-office features | v1.2+ — no point building features before the founding cohort is live |
