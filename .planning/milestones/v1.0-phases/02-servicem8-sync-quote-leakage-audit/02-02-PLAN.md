## Phase Goal

**As a** the operator, **I want to** have every connected client's quote changes detected within one polling interval and turned into consumable events, **so that** the cadence engine can act on wins, losses, and new quotes without ever re-checking ServiceM8 itself.

<objective>
Build the sync engine core on top of the 02-01 tracer: the remaining sync tables (contacts, the SYNC-02 transactional outbox, sync-run log, operator allowlist for non-tenant tables), the three pure policy modules (cursor per Pattern 1, transitions per Pattern 3, failure classification per D-06), and the per-connection orchestrator `runSyncTick` that plan 02-03 exposes over the tick route.

Purpose: this is the "trustworthy state" heart of the phase — every guarantee here (cursor never skips, events never fire falsely, pauses always recorded) is a mechanism with a test, per the RESEARCH key insight.
Output: runnable `runSyncTick()` with events, logging, and failure policy — everything except the HTTP/cron wiring.

Requirements: SYNC-01 (cursor polling), SYNC-02 (status-transition events), SYNC-04 (failure classification + pause flag). Decisions: D-06, D-13 (budget math honoured), D-16. RESEARCH Patterns 1, 3, 5.
</objective>
