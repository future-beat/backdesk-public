<objective>
Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Every recovered dollar is attributed with an evidence chain and reported in the client's language — the invoice arrives stapled to proof it paid for itself."

This plan writes every RULE that goal depends on as pure, framework-free code provable on bare `npm test`: the five constants figure and footnote both derive from (D-01/D-08/D-16/D-18), the D-02 qualifying-touch predicate and the D-01 window, the D-06/D-08 baseline resolver, the D-07 conservative haircut in integer basis points, the D-05 evidence chain schema, one composed `decideAttribution` decision, calendar-month bounds in the tenant zone (D-10), fee pro-rating (D-13), the report figures (RPT-02) and the five-plus-one instrumentation metrics (RPT-03) through one derivation.

Purpose: the consumer (07-03), the generator (07-04) and both instrumentation pages (07-05) do IO and then call these functions; nothing downstream re-derives a rule, so the row, the report and the screen cannot disagree.
Output: `src/core/attribution/{qualify,baseline,money,evidence,decide}.ts`, `src/core/reporting/{period,fees,figures,metrics,format}.ts`, the extended constants module, seven pure suites and the extended constants pin.
