<objective>
Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Every recovered dollar is attributed with an evidence chain and reported in the client's language — the invoice arrives stapled to proof it paid for itself."

This plan delivers RPT-02: the operator picks a month on `/reports/[client]`, the server writes a frozen versioned `monthly_reports` row (figures, per-job lines, baseline, constants, fees), and `/reports/[client]/[reportId]` renders that row as the A4 document the operator prints to PDF and attaches to the invoice — with the fee schedule that supplies the multiple's denominator edited on the same page (UI-SPEC U-1).

Purpose: the document that will be challenged by the person being billed; every number on it must trace to a row the client recognises and to a footnote computed from the same value.
Output: `generate.ts`, `copy.ts`, per-tenant count reads, the `/reports/[client]` page (Monthly reports + Fee schedule), two server actions, two client forms, the printed page + `print-button.tsx` + `report.css`, `.num`/`.metrics` globals, three suites and two pinned-test extensions.
