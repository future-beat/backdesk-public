<objective>
Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "Every recovered dollar is attributed with an evidence chain and reported in the client's language — the invoice arrives stapled to proof it paid for itself."

This plan makes RPT-01 happen in production: a second outbox consumer, cursor `'attribution'`, that walks `quote_status_events` in id order inside the per-minute cadence tick, asks `decideAttribution` (07-02) about every `won` event, writes at most one `quote_attributions` row per quote through the 07-01 claim, and advances its own cursor in the same transaction — with the tick seam that keeps every existing remote suite from attributing a real client's history.

Purpose: D-04/D-05 are only facts if a row exists to be unique and to carry the chain; report-time computation was rejected (RESEARCH Summary). The consumer is the thing that turns preserved evidence into a billable record.
Output: `src/core/attribution/consume-wins.ts`, three evidence reads on `forTenant()`, the `attribution` seam + six counts on the tick and its route, the pinned-test updates, one remote suite and one pure isolation suite.
</objective>
