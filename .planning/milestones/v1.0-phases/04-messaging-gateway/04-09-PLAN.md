repo's own live capture proves carry no verdict ('Sent'/'Failed', '201'/'301'; the verdict prose

calls performSends OUTSIDE the per-tenant try/catch (dispatch.ts:537, :578). One transient driver
error abandons the current tenant's remaining claimed sends at outcome IS NULL forever (at-most-once
means never auto-resent — D-07) and skips every remaining tenant, contradicting the loop's own
documented invariant.

Purpose: the compliance backstop must actually fire (D-03's over-suppression direction requires the
evidence to exist), and one client's failure must not stop the other fourteen.
Output: shared verdict-corpus builder, live-shaped fixtures, two isolation guards, and a mocked
failure-injection suite.
