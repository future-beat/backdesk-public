## Phase Goal

**As a** the operator, **I want to** trust that an overdue touch moves to the next polite slot or gets skipped — and that no race, replay, or stale queue entry can ever double-text a customer, **so that** the mechanics can never make my client look spammy.

- run-tick progress pass: promotion + re-window + expiry sweep wired between consume and dispatch

## Flagged assumptions (edge probe)

- CAD-02 carried an unclassified edge-probe row.
