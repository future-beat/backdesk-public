# Requirements: Backdesk — v1.2 Missed-call text-back

**Defined:** 2026-09-27
**Core Value:** Every open quote gets followed up on time, with a human-approved message, and every recovered job is attributed and reported in dollars — the invoice must arrive stapled to proof it paid for itself.

**Milestone goal:** Every missed inbound call to a client's diverted number gets a text within seconds, becomes a human-supervised lead that is chased briefly, escalated when hot and written into ServiceM8 on intent — and the whole thing produces Backdesk's first-party missed-call data. Stage 0 of `docs/reports/Avoca AI and an Australian equivalent.md`. Research: `.planning/research/SUMMARY.md` (decisions S1–S19, rules R1–R7).

**Founder decisions (27 Sep 2026):** v1.1 closed with its two live proofs carried forward; lead cadence = one instant text + two follow-ups, then closed for good; a missed caller with an open quote cadence at the same client gets no new lead; optional scope in = URGENT keyword + per-client onboarding runbook; out = service-area check, operator spam tags, client-reported call totals.

## v1.2 Requirements

### Capture (CAP) — the diverted call becomes a logged missed call

- [x] **CAP-01**: Operator can provision one Australian local (08) Twilio number per client from the console, recorded against the tenant, so the client can divert no-answer, busy and unreachable calls to it; numbers are never shared between clients and never released while the client is active
- [x] **CAP-02**: A diverted call to a client's number is answered with a static, unrecorded greeting (≤ 12 s: the tradie's business name, that a text is on its way, that the call is not recorded, and that private-number callers should text instead) and then hung up; no `<Record>`, transcription or voicemail exists anywhere in the flow, asserted by a test
- [x] **CAP-03**: Every diverted call — including withheld, landline and international callers — is stored as a `missed_calls` row (tenant from the called number, caller CLI or a withheld marker, start time in Perth time, after-hours flag, provider call id) idempotent on the provider call id, captured from the signed provider webhook and reconciled from the provider call log so a dropped webhook cannot lose a call
- [x] **CAP-04**: The provider webhooks verify the provider signature over the canonical public URL and a per-route token before any write, never return 5xx to the provider, and log a fixed-string failure that the ops probe can find
- [x] **CAP-05**: Operator can see, per client, that the divert is healthy: a verification call is observed and recorded before text-back can be armed, and the console shows the days since the last diverted call

### Text-back (TXT) — the instant text and who gets it

- [x] **TXT-01**: Within seconds of the missed call, the caller receives one SMS from the client's identity through the existing shared number, built from the client's pre-approved static template (business-hours and after-hours variants), ≤ 2 GSM segments, with the Spam Act identity line and STOP; no LLM is in this path
- [x] **TXT-02**: The text-back is sent through the existing send ledger (claim → sent → provider outcome) so delivery receipts, reconcile, STOP suppression and metrics apply to it exactly as to a cadence touch, with the per-minute tick as the guarantee when the synchronous send fails
- [x] **TXT-03**: A deterministic gate decides who is texted, before any send: suppressed numbers, withheld/anonymous callers, non-Australian-mobile numbers, the client's own numbers and a per-client never-text list are skipped and the skip reason is recorded on the missed call; one text per caller per client per 24 hours; a per-client daily cap (default 30) stops call floods
- [x] **TXT-04**: A caller who already has an open quote cadence at the same client gets no new lead: the missed call attaches to that cadence, the cadence pauses, the caller receives the client's "about your quote" template, and the tradie's escalation phone is alerted (founder decision D-16, 6 Oct 2026; was "the operator is escalated")
- [x] **TXT-05**: Text-back is armed per client through the existing arming gate, additionally requiring the divert verification call (CAP-05), an attested text-back template set and an entry in the never-text list for the client's own numbers; the go-live checklist shows the item, and disarming stops text-backs immediately
- [x] **TXT-06**: The operator receives an alert when the ClickSend balance falls below a floor and text-back auto-disarms below a lower floor, so a failed text-back can never be silent; any failed text-back is surfaced to the operator within the tick

### Leads (LEAD) — the short supervised cadence

- [x] **LEAD-01**: Each texted caller becomes a lead with its own cadence of kind `lead`: the instant text is touch 1, exempt from contact hours; touches 2 and 3 are AI-drafted from approved lead templates, human-approved in the existing queue, materialised inside contact hours over about 48 hours; after touch 3 the lead closes for good and is never texted again
- [x] **LEAD-02**: Replies to a lead are classified into a separate lead vocabulary (new_enquiry, callback_request, question, existing_job, complaint, not_a_lead, already_sorted, spam, opt_out, other) with an urgency level and confidence; STOP is handled deterministically before any model, exactly as today
- [x] **LEAD-03**: Hot leads reach the tradie's phone through the existing escalation path: any emergency-urgency reply, a high-confidence enquiry, callback, existing-job or complaint, a deterministic URGENT keyword reply, and a second missed call from the same caller within 30 minutes; after-hours escalation is a per-client switch
- [x] **LEAD-04**: A reply of DONE (or a chosen keyword) from the tradie's own number closes the lead, and a lead whose caller mobile appears on a ServiceM8 job created within 14 days is auto-resolved as "booked by the tradie", so leads-to-jobs is honest without operator work
- [x] **LEAD-05**: Cadence policy (minimum gap, staleness cap, expiry) is chosen per cadence kind, so the lead cadence's short timings cannot be skipped by the quote cadence's 48-hour rules, pinned by a test on both kinds

### ServiceM8 write (SM8) — the job on confirmed intent

- [x] **SM8-01**: From a lead with confirmed intent, the operator can create a ServiceM8 job with one tap: status Quote, the caller's own words as the description, the company matched by mobile or created, and a job contact; the job is created through a new POST path in the ServiceM8 client that verifies the created record by reading it back
- [x] **SM8-02**: A job created from a lead is pre-inserted into the quotes mirror as `needs_review` with source `lead`, so the sync's first-sight rule does not open a second quote cadence on a customer the lead cadence just chased
- [x] **SM8-03**: Job creation is idempotent per lead (a second tap cannot create a second job) and failures leave the lead in a state the operator can retry, with the ServiceM8 error kind recorded

### Console (CON) — operating it from a phone

- [x] **CON-05**: Operator can work a cross-client lead queue sorted by deadline, in the same shape as the approval queue, showing the caller's mobile, when they rang, after-hours flag, the latest classified reply with urgency, and the next action (approve touch, create job, close)
- [x] **CON-06**: Operator can see a per-client missed-call log (every diverted call, whether it was texted and why not, and the lead it opened) and the client's divert number, template set and text-back arming state on the client page

### Reporting (RPT) — first-party numbers

- [x] **RPT-04**: The monthly report and the `/reports` instrumentation show, per client, missed calls captured (with hour-of-day and weekday split), after-hours share, median and P90 time from call arrival to text delivered, reply rate on a delivered denominator, and leads → jobs created → jobs won; rates are shown only with their denominators, and lead-born jobs are counted, not attributed dollars, in v1.2
- [x] **RPT-05**: Operator can export a design-partner stats CSV (the same figures, per client, per month) so first-party missed-call data can replace vendor statistics in the sales material

### Compliance and onboarding (COMP)

- [x] **COMP-01**: The privacy notice gains a callers section (what is captured from a diverted call, who processes it, retention) and the first text carries the plain-text privacy pointer; no link is sent in the first text
- [x] **COMP-02**: Closed non-converting missed-call rows are pseudonymised after 90 days while suppressions are kept forever, by a scheduled job with a read-back test
- [x] **COMP-03**: A per-client onboarding runbook covers the carrier divert codes (61, 62, 67; iPhone settings caveat), the verification call, the carrier-charge warning, a holiday pause state and offboarding (remove the divert before the number is retired)

## Out of Scope

- Voice answering, call recording, voicemail-to-text, IVR — Stage 1, not v1.2 (decision 27 Sep 2026; WA all-party consent stays untouched because nothing is recorded)
- LLM-drafted instant text — the "within seconds" promise and human supervision are only compatible with a fixed, attested template
- Auto-created ServiceM8 jobs without operator approval; drips longer than 1 + 2 touches; links in the first text; texting the tradie on every miss
- A shared divert number across clients — `ForwardedFrom` is carrier-dependent, so tenant identity must come from the called number
- Voice-based outbound call-backs — DNCR/Telemarketing Standard overhead (Stage 3, only if pulled)
- Marketing execution (Cowork thread)
