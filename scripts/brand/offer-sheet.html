Backdesk

Missed-call text-back for Perth trade businesses. Every missed call texted back in seconds. Every quote chased until it's won or dead.

For Perth trade businesses that can't answer every call, Backdesk is a done-for-you back desk. When you can't pick up, the call diverts to us, the caller gets one text from your business within seconds, and we follow up until the job is booked. Every quote you send is chased by text and email until it's won or dead. Run by AI, checked by a human, reported in dollars. If the recovered quote value doesn't beat 3× the fee in 90 days, you don't pay until it does.

How it works

1. Free Quote Leakage AuditOne A4 page from your own ServiceM8 data: quotes sent in the last 90 days, value never followed up, estimated recoverable range. You see the number before you decide.
2. Set up in 7 daysWe connect to ServiceM8, give you a Backdesk number your phone diverts to when you can't answer (nothing is recorded), and write the text-back and follow-up templates in your voice. You approve every one.
3. Every missed call texted backOne text within seconds of the missed call, from your business, then two human-approved follow-ups inside business hours until the job is booked or the lead is closed. Urgent and hot replies go to your phone straight away. A repeat caller is never texted twice.
4. Every open quote chased, reported in dollarsFive touches over three weeks, SMS and email, every message checked by a person before it sends, with opt-out on every one. One page a month: calls caught, leads, jobs booked, quotes chased, revenue recovered, fees paid, multiple returned.

The guarantee, precisely

If the recovered quote value we attribute in your first 90 days is not more than 3× the monthly fees you paid in that period, the service is free until it is. Attribution is agreed before we start: a job counts when it is won after one of our follow-ups, and every report shows the figure two ways, gross and conservative (net of the win rate you already had before we connected). We deliberately count low.

Terms

Month to month, no lock-in. Billed monthly by card from day 31. Cancel any time in writing, effective at the end of the month, from either side. Prices exclude GST. The client agreement is in plain English and you keep the data.

Your customers, your name

  • Texts and follow-ups come from your business, in your voice, using templates you approved. Prices and dates are taken from ServiceM8 exactly; a message that changes them does not send.
  • Every SMS and email identifies your business and carries an opt-out. Follow-ups stay inside business hours, Perth time; only the instant text-back goes out whenever the call came in. Any customer can be stopped the same day, by you or by them.
  • Every message is drafted by AI and checked by a person before it goes, and that is disclosed. Complaint and opt-out rates are tracked as a first-class number; above 5% of contacts we stop and call you.
  • Nothing is recorded on the call. Your quote data, call records and our logins are held in Sydney; email and AI providers process message content outside Australia, SMS goes through an Australian provider. Full privacy notice at backdesk.com.au/privacy.
